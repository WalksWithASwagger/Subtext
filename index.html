A chart of a sentence

Ask a small language model something. At every token, Subtext reads the model's layers and charts what it has on its mind.

  1. The conversation runs along the top like a coastline: your words, then its reply.
  2. Underneath are the model's layers. Deep water is early layers, still taking your words in. Shallow water is late layers, about to speak.
  3. Italic words are what it has on its mind at that token. Held across tokens, a word becomes a current.
  4. A current that turns red and rises was thought ahead: on its mind before it said it. +21 means 21 tokens early.
  5. A wreck mark is a word it held strongly and never said. That's the subtext.

Drag or scroll along the chart, hover to peek at any token, click a current to follow it. Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys step, 1 2 3 switch lenses, + and − zoom, N turns the chart to night. This shows what information is present inside the model, not what it feels.
